\documentclass[11pt]{letter}
\usepackage{geometry}
\usepackage[seed=1203]{kacho}
\signature{Hana Takeda}
\address{12 Garden Lane\\ Kamakura}
\begin{document}
\begin{letter}{Mr.\ Kenji Morita\\ 3 Temple Road\\ Kyoto}
\opening{Dear Kenji,}
Thank you for the parcel of plum branches, which arrived in perfect
condition. They are now standing in the tall celadon vase by the window and
the first buds opened this morning. The scent fills the whole room, and in
the late afternoon light the shadows of the twigs fall across the paper
screens like brush strokes.

I have been practising the bamboo exercises you showed me. The nodes are
still too heavy and the leaves look more like fish than like the quick
strokes of the masters, but I persevere. Perhaps when you visit in spring we
can paint together under the cherry trees at the shrine.

Please give my regards to your family.
\closing{With warm wishes,}
\ps{P.S. The narcissus bulbs are already sprouting.}
\end{letter}
\end{document}
